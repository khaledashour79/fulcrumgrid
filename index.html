Why one platform

The grid holds it all together

Separate tools create silos, double entry, and blind spots. FulcrumGrid gives every app a shared foundation — so your data, identity, and access stay in sync.

One secure identity

Single sign-on and role-based permissions across every app. Grant the right access once, everywhere.

Shared data layer

Customers, employees, and transactions live once. Update in one app and it's current everywhere.

Consistent experience

One design language, one navigation. Teams learn the platform once and move fast in every app.

Automations that span apps

Trigger a collection task from a Command Center alert, or onboard from HR in a click. Connect the flow.

Analytics across the board

Report on the whole business, not one tool at a time. Every app feeds the same source of truth.

Grows with you

Start with one app. Add the rest as you scale — no migration, no re-platforming, no rip and replace.
