FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about what FulcrumGrid is, what it includes, and how it works. Still have a question? Just ask.

What is FulcrumGrid?

FulcrumGrid is a family of purpose-built business apps that run on one platform — Command Center (a real-time operations dashboard), Collection (accounts receivable and payments), and HR Suite (people operations) — plus custom apps built for a specific workflow. It is built by Avenlor Consulting.

Which apps does FulcrumGrid include?

Available now: Command Center (operations dashboard), Collection (receivables and payments), and HR Suite (people operations). Inventory, CRM, Analytics, and Procurement are on the roadmap, and we also build custom apps on the same platform.

Who is FulcrumGrid for?

FulcrumGrid is built for small and mid-sized businesses and the operations, finance, and people teams that keep them running day to day.

Can I start with just one app?

Yes. You can start with a single app and add the rest whenever you’re ready — everything runs on the same platform, so there’s no migration or re-platforming. See pricing.

Does FulcrumGrid support Arabic?

Yes. FulcrumGrid is fully bilingual, with complete English and Arabic (right-to-left) versions of the product and site. See the Arabic site.

How much does FulcrumGrid cost?

FulcrumGrid uses simple per-user, per-month plans — Starter, Business, and Enterprise — and every plan includes a free trial. See the pricing page for current details.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every plan includes a 14-day free trial with full features, and no credit card is required to start.

Can I export my data?

Yes. Every app keeps clean, structured records you can export at any time. Your data stays yours.

How does FulcrumGrid handle access and security?

Each app has role-based access and a full audit trail, so you can grant the right people the right access and see the history of changes.

Can FulcrumGrid build a custom app for my workflow?

Yes. Beyond the ready-made apps, we build customized business apps tailored to your specific use case, on the same secure, auditable platform as the rest of the grid.

How do I get started or request a demo?

Tell us what your team needs on the contact page, or email contact@avenlorconsulting.com and we’ll show you FulcrumGrid in action.

Who builds FulcrumGrid?

FulcrumGrid is built and supported by Avenlor Consulting.
