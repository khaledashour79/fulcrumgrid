How this is calculated

Annual activity volumes. CV screening = annual hires × applications per vacancy. Initial interviews = annual hires × initial interviews per hire. Payroll = headcount × 12 cycles. Iqama renewals = renewals entered. Letters = monthly requests × 12.

Per enabled activity. Annual hours released = volume × (manual minutes − platform minutes) ÷ 60 × realization factor. Hourly staff cost = fully loaded monthly cost ÷ productive monthly hours. Annual capacity value = hours released × hourly cost. Monthly = annual ÷ 12.

CV screening and interview review are calculated separately and combined under Recruitment. We deliberately exclude blanket productivity percentages, avoided fines, hiring-quality gains, payroll-error savings, and approval-chasing or accounting work — those could overlap with the four feature groups.
