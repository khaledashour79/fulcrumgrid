02 · Capabilities

Governance, risk and compliance in one system.

Every obligation mapped to a control, every control backed by evidence — the whole GRC cycle, tracked and enforced.

Saudi regulatory catalog

Obligations arrive pre-mapped for NCA ECC-2, PDPL, CMA Corporate Governance and the Companies Law, alongside ISO 27001 and NIST CSF — applied per legal entity by a scope gate.

Multi-entity scope gate

Register every legal entity, and the scope gate decides which obligations apply to each from its attributes — so a group assesses once and applies the result across all its entities.

Recurring obligation engine

Compliance tasks are generated automatically on both the Hijri and Gregorian calendars, so nothing lapses between reviews.

Maker-checker workflow

Submit, review, approve: every piece of evidence moves through a segregation of duties the database enforces, not just the screen.

Immutable evidence vault

Every uploaded file is content-hashed on arrival, so it can't be altered or deleted after the fact.

Controls & crosswalk

Rate each control's implementation and maturity, and map one control to several frameworks so the same evidence is reused, not re-collected.

Risk register

Score risks inherent and residual, attach treatment plans, and time-box risk acceptance so it auto-reopens when it expires.

Authorized attack-surface scanning

Outside-in scanning gated by signed authorization, asset-ownership checks and a kill switch, so security testing stays in scope — findings carry remediation SLAs by severity.

Cloud posture (CSPM)

Read-only connections to AWS, Azure and GCP run scheduled cloud-posture checks — no static keys stored — and raise findings against your controls.

PDPL privacy toolkit

Handle data-subject requests (DSAR) and keep Records of Processing Activities (RoPA), with DPIA flags where processing needs a closer look.

Vendor risk (TPRM)

A third-party register with risk tiers, data-shared flags and a reassessment cadence, so vendor risk stays current.

Incident management

Track severity and timeline, with a regulatory-notification deadline clock running from the moment an incident is logged.

Attestations

Run sign-off campaigns that collect signatures and issue certificates when each attestation completes.

My Work inbox

A personal queue that shows each user exactly the obligations, reviews and attestations awaiting their action — nothing to hunt for.

Executive dashboard & KRIs

Board-ready indicators — open critical findings, SLA breaches, overdue obligations, compliance % and open incidents — with posture tracked day over day.

Reports & exports

Compliance-status and audit-pack exports to CSV, plus print-ready reports to hand an auditor or the board.

Audit-ready & secure

An append-only audit trail, CSV and PDF reports, OIDC single sign-on, API tokens and per-tenant isolation with row-level security — in Arabic and English.
