02 · Capabilities

The whole group, read from the books.

Commercial and financial figures only — no HR — consolidated across every company you connect.

One dashboard, every company

Revenue, collections, cash, receivables and payables for each company you run — side by side and consolidated into one view.

AR ageing

Open receivables bucketed by age, so you can see what's current and what's slipping — per company and across the group.

P&L snapshot

Expenses, other income and a net figure read straight from your books, alongside revenue and cash.

VAT & ZATCA status

VAT derived from your invoices and purchases, with each company's ZATCA e-invoicing status surfaced (best-effort).

Recurring revenue & pipeline

MRR from subscriptions and an estimates-to-invoices conversion view, where your books carry them.

Client insight

Client counts, new-versus-returning cohorts and your top clients by value, drawn from invoice history.

Base-currency consolidation

Each company keeps its own currency; you set a base currency and FX rates, and mixed-currency entities roll up into one comparable total.

Per-company drill-in

A companies table that opens any single entity's detail — from the group headline down to one company's figures.

Nightly snapshots

A worker pulls each company's figures on a schedule; the dashboard reads those snapshots, so it loads fast and never hammers your accounting system live.

Encrypted keys, read-only

API keys are AES-256-GCM encrypted at rest and never sent back to the browser. Consolidate only reads your figures — it never writes to your books.

Hardened connections

Every data pull is host-pinned with SSRF protection, rejected redirects and strict size and time limits, behind an optional operator token.

Install it like an app

An installable progressive web app with an offline fallback and opt-in push that tells you when a fresh snapshot is in.
