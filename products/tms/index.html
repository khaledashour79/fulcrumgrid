02 · Capabilities

Run the operation and the finance on one system.

Twelve capabilities, from the shipment file to the general ledger.

Built-in finance

A native double-entry general ledger with AR, AP, bank reconciliation and job-level P&L, so operations and accounting live on one system.

VAT engine & returns

Output, input and import VAT kept as distinct accounts, with VAT-return preparation.

Freight forwarding

Shipment files, cargo and containers, transport documents, tariffs and demurrage and detention — with GCC seaports, airports and border crossings built in.

Customs clearance

Declarations with customs duty kept separate from import VAT, and claimant records.

Transport & fleet

Container and cargo job orders, legs, carriers — your own or a vendor's — and a driver app.

Container yard & depot

Gate-in and gate-out, occupancy, zones, multi-yard and storage billing — with a 3D yard view.

Parcel & express

Booking, hubs, first and last mile, cash-on-delivery, an SLA clock, a rate engine and tracking.

Warehousing & 3PL

Addressable storage, putaway, pick, pack and ship, cycle counts and storage billing.

Purchasing & inventory

Purchase orders, goods receipts, stock valuation and invoice matching.

Maintenance & workshop

Assets, work orders, preventive maintenance, and parts and labour.

ERP connectors

Two-way connectors for SAP Business One, Odoo and Oracle (Fusion and OIC), plus GPS and telematics, SMS, OIDC single sign-on, webhooks and a REST API.

Multi-tenant & multi-currency

Strict per-tenant isolation with row-level security, every amount in its own ISO currency, and Arabic and English with full right-to-left.

04 · Your region

Arabic & English

VAT, kept straight
