Capabilities

Everything you need to run operations live

From high-level health to the single alert that needs attention right now.

Live dashboards

Build dashboards from any data on the grid. Metrics refresh in real time — no exports, no stale reports.

Alerts & thresholds

Set thresholds on any metric and get notified the moment they're crossed — in-app, by email, or to your team channel.

Automated workflows

Turn a signal into an action: open a task, notify an owner, or trigger a job in another app — automatically.

Cross-app reporting

Report across Collection, HR Suite, and every other app at once — one source of truth, not a dozen spreadsheets.

Custom views & KPIs

Define the KPIs that matter to your business and pin them into role-specific views for each team.

Audit trail

Every change, alert, and action is logged. Know what happened, when, and who acted on it.
