02 · Capabilities

Everything you need to run operations live.

From high-level health to the single alert that needs attention right now.

Live dashboards

Build dashboards from any data on the grid. Metrics refresh in real time — no exports, no stale reports.

Alerts & thresholds

Set thresholds on any metric and get notified the moment they're crossed — in-app, by email, or to your team channel.

Automated workflows

Turn a signal into an action: open a task, notify an owner, or trigger a job in another app — automatically.

Reporting & exports

Build the reports your team needs and export them anytime — no more digging through a dozen spreadsheets.

Custom views & KPIs

Define the KPIs that matter to your business and pin them into role-specific views for each team.

Audit trail

Every change, alert, and action is logged. Know what happened, when, and who acted on it.

03 · Integrations

Connects to your ERP and accounting.

Feed live balances into your dashboards without double entry. All integrations →

SAP Business One

Two-way sync for business partners, items and journal entries — no re-keying, and balances land straight in Command Center.

Odoo

A two-way connector that keeps contacts, invoices and payments in step between Odoo and the grid.

Oracle

Stream live balances and receivables from Oracle financials into your dashboards and alerts.

QuickBooks Online

Link QuickBooks Online (QBO) to match invoices to payments and reconcile automatically.

One connection on Growth

Growth includes a single ERP connection; Enterprise unlocks unlimited connections with a scheduled pull.

Scheduled pull

Let Command Center pull records from your ERP on a schedule, so dashboards stay current on their own.
