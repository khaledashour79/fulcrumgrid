02 · Capabilities

IFRS estimates, built to be proven.

Every period-end calculation, from a frozen input to a signed-off audit pack.

IFRS period-end engines

IAS 19 end-of-service benefits, IFRS 9 expected credit loss and IFRS 16 leases, in one workspace.

Controlled close

Open, review and lock each reporting period; reopening needs a second approver and a logged reason.

Frozen input snapshots

Source data is validated and frozen with a content hash, so a later change to the source never alters a past result.

Versioned assumptions with evidence

Every discount rate or growth assumption carries a rationale and an attached evidence file, and must be approved before it's used.

Reproducible runs

Every calculation re-runs to a byte-identical result hash, provable to an auditor.

Segregation of duties

The same person can't both prepare and approve an estimate.

External actuary sign-off

IAS 19 runs carry a dedicated actuary review and sign-off stage.

Tracked adjustments

Manual overrides are recorded as separate lines with a reason and an approver, never typed over the computed figures.

Draft journal vouchers

Balanced, tie-to-results journals exported as CSV or Excel for your ERP.

One-click audit pack

A bilingual Excel pack per run — inputs, assumptions, workings, sensitivities and the sign-off trail — with a hash manifest.

PDPL-grade access control

Every view, export or download of personal data is gated and written to an append-only access log.

Arabic-first, RTL

The whole workspace is built right-to-left, with full Arabic and English and multi-currency (ISO-4217, SAR default).
