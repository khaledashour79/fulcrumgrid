FAQ

Pricing questions, answered

Can I start with just one app?

Yes. The Starter plan is designed for a single app — pick Command Center, Collection, or HR Suite and add the rest whenever you're ready.

Can I add apps later?

Absolutely. Everything runs on one platform and shares a data layer, so adding an app is instant — no migration or re-implementation.

Is there a free trial?

Every plan includes a 14-day free trial with full features. No credit card required to start.

How does billing work?

Plans are billed per user, per month. Switch to annual billing to save 20%. You can change plans or user counts at any time.

Do you offer discounts?

We offer special pricing for nonprofits, educational institutions, and early-stage startups. Reach out and we'll help find the right fit.
