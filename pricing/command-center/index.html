FAQ

Command Center pricing, answered

Can I change plans later?

Yes — move up or down between Starter, Pro, Business, and Enterprise at any time. Changes take effect on your next billing cycle.

How are users counted?

A user is anyone with a login to Command Center. You're billed per active user, per month.

Do I need other FulcrumGrid apps?

No. Command Center works on its own. If you run several apps, the whole-grid bundle is cheaper than subscribing to each.

Is there a free trial?

Every plan includes a 14-day free trial with full features. No credit card required to start.
