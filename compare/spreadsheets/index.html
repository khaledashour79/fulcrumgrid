When a spreadsheet is still the right tool

Spreadsheets are excellent for quick models, throwaway calculations, and solo one-off analysis — fast, flexible, and familiar. FulcrumGrid isn't trying to replace that. The switch pays off once several people share the file, it becomes the system of record for a process, or a single wrong cell starts costing real money.

Common questions

Is FulcrumGrid a spreadsheet replacement?

For running a business process — invoicing, HR records, operations tracking — yes. FulcrumGrid replaces the fragile spreadsheets teams outgrow with structured, multi-user apps. For quick one-off analysis, a spreadsheet is still a fine tool.

Can I import my existing spreadsheets?

Yes. You can bring your records in when you set up an app. Tell us what you have on the contact page and we’ll help you move.

When is a spreadsheet still the better choice?

For a quick model, a throwaway calculation, or a solo one-off analysis, a spreadsheet is fast and flexible. The moment several people share it, it becomes a system of record, or mistakes start to cost money, a purpose-built app pays off.
