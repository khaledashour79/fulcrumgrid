Integrations

Connects to the systems you already run.

FulcrumGrid works alongside your ERP and accounting stack — SAP Business One, Odoo, Oracle and QuickBooks Online — and gives your developers a REST API, webhooks and SSO/SCIM to wire it into everything else. Keep your books where they are; put your operations on one grid.

Talk to us about your stack Explore the apps

01 · ERP & accounting

Connect your ERP and accounting.

Sync master data, post transactions, and pull balances on a schedule — without double entry.

SAP Business One

Two-way sync for business partners, items and journal entries — a change on either side lands on the other, with no re-keying. Available across HR Suite, Collection and Command Center.

Odoo

Post HR Suite payroll as draft journal entries into Odoo, and keep Command Center dashboards reading from the same numbers — a two-way connection, with no re-keying.

Oracle

Command Center streams live Oracle balances into your dashboards, and HR Suite posts payroll journals through Oracle Integration (OIC) and Oracle Fusion (ERP Cloud).

QuickBooks Online

Link QuickBooks Online (QBO) to match invoices to payments and reconcile automatically. Your books stay in QuickBooks; the export-and-reimport busywork disappears.

Command Center includes one ERP connection on Growth, and unlimited connections with a scheduled pull on Enterprise. See Command Center integrations →

02 · Build on the API

Wire it into everything else.

Everything you can do in the apps, you can do through the API — with a sandbox to build against safely.

REST API v1

A clean, versioned REST API with predictable JSON and token auth — read and write any record your team can reach in the app. Fully documented: Collection's REST API on Business and up, and HR Suite's Partner API on Enterprise.

Webhooks

Subscribe to the events that matter and FulcrumGrid pushes them to your endpoints the moment they happen — so your own systems react in real time, with no polling and no delay.

Sandbox

A full sandbox that mirrors production, with its own keys and test data — build and validate every integration safely before a single live record is touched.

Scheduled pull

Set a cadence and Command Center pulls balances and records from your ERP on its own — hourly, nightly, however you need — so dashboards stay current without anyone lifting a finger.

03 · Identity & access

Single sign-on and provisioning.

Bring your own identity provider and manage people the way you already do.

SSO

Single sign-on through the identity provider you already run, so your team signs in once with credentials IT already controls — no extra passwords to manage. Available on HR Suite and Collection (Enterprise).

SCIM

Automated provisioning through SCIM — new hires get the right access the day they start, and leavers lose it the moment they're offboarded, straight from your directory.

04 · Alerts & messaging

Push the signal where your team already is.

Send what matters to the channels people already watch — no extra dashboard to check.

Slack & Microsoft Teams

Command Center posts a summary of overdue items and key events to a Slack or Teams channel through an incoming webhook — so your team sees what needs attention without opening the app.

Multi-channel reminders

Collection reaches each debtor on the channel they actually answer, sent automatically on the reminder schedule you set — so follow-ups go out without anyone chasing by hand.
