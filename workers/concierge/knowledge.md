**Company.** FulcrumGrid is a growing family of purpose-built business apps from one
team, made by **Avenlor Consulting**. Site: **fulcrumgrid.com**. Taglines: "The
operational backbone for modern teams" and "One grid. Every operation." Contact / sales:
**contact@avenlorconsulting.com** or the demo form at **/contact/**. Pricing at **/pricing/**.
Start with one app today and add the rest as you grow — every app runs on the same
secure, auditable grid, with cross-app data when you run several.

**Live apps (6).**
- **HR Suite** — people operations from hire to retire: employee records, onboarding,
  payroll (with GOSI, end-of-service/EOSB and Nitaqat/Saudization tracking for Saudi),
  time off, attendance, performance (incl. PIPs), recruitment (ATS) with one-way video
  interviews and live interview scheduling (Google Meet/Teams/Zoom), learning, credentials
  & renewals (Iqama/licence expiry), recognition, loans & advances, total-rewards
  statements, medical-insurance membership administration, confidential (optionally
  anonymous) grievances, and offboarding with exit interviews, clearance and access cutoff.
  For Saudi/GCC it also runs in-house government workflows — **Iqama renewal cycles, exit &
  re-entry / final-exit visas, and overseas hiring via block-visa pools and offshore
  recruitment agencies** (fee pricing, approvals, credential write-back; government fines
  imported by CSV — it prepares and records, it never files with the government on your
  behalf), plus GCC home-leave air tickets. Data-subject rights (DSAR access/erasure with a
  retention classification, for PDPL/GDPR). Posts draft journal vouchers to SAP Business One
  or Daftara and GL entries to FulcrumGrid TMS. 50+ modules on one grid (Core HR always on),
  AI Copilot & human-approved agents, a partner REST API, and OIDC SSO / SCIM.
  **From SAR 5 / seat / month** (Starter; Growth SAR 10, Enterprise SAR 18, per seat/mo).
- **Command Center** — a real-time operations dashboard / control room: live KPIs and
  custom dashboards, alerts and thresholds, automated workflows, reporting and exports,
  OKRs/projects/approvals, and ERP integrations. **From SAR 490 / month** (Pilot, flat per
  organization; Growth SAR 1,900/mo, Enterprise custom).
- **Collection** — receivables and payments, handled: invoice and ledger tracking,
  automated reminders and payment plans, reconciliation, cheque register, ERP connectors,
  aging and risk reports. **Starter is free**, then **from SAR 370 / month**
  (Professional; Business SAR 1,120/mo, Enterprise/Agency custom).
- **TMS** — a logistics-operations and finance suite for GCC SMEs: freight forwarding,
  customs clearance, transport & fleet, container yard/depot, parcel & express,
  warehousing/3PL, purchasing & inventory, maintenance & workshop, with a built-in
  double-entry general ledger (AR, AP, bank reconciliation, VAT engine). ERP connectors,
  Arabic + English, multi-currency. **Pricing is custom — talk to sales.**
- **Assure** — a governance, risk & compliance (GRC) platform built for Saudi regulation:
  NCA ECC-2, PDPL, CMA and Companies Law obligations mapped to controls with a multi-entity
  scope gate, a maker-checker evidence workflow, immutable evidence vault, risk register,
  authorized attack-surface scanning and multi-cloud posture (CSPM), vendor risk, PDPL
  DSAR + RoPA, incidents, attestations, board-ready KRIs and print-ready audit reports.
  **Pricing is custom — talk to sales.**
- **Close** — IFRS period-end estimates: IAS 19 end-of-service, IFRS 9 expected credit loss
  and IFRS 16 leases, with controlled periods, frozen snapshots, versioned assumptions,
  reproducible runs, segregation of duties, draft journal vouchers and a bilingual audit
  pack. For Saudi finance teams preparing IFRS. **Pricing is custom — talk to sales.**
- **Consolidate** — operator dashboard that consolidates commercial & financial figures
  across every company you run: revenue, cash, AR ageing, P&L, VAT and ZATCA status, MRR,
  pipeline and client cohorts, read from your books as nightly snapshots (read-only, never
  writes back). Connected to **Daftra** today; SAP Business One, Odoo and Oracle are on the
  way behind the same connector. Multi-currency with base-currency roll-up; encrypted keys,
  hardened read-only access. **Pricing is custom — talk to sales.**

**Bundle.** Running several apps? The **whole-grid** plan covers every app at **$29 /
user / month** (unlimited users, cross-app data, priority support); **Enterprise** is
custom (single-tenant, on-premise/self-hosted option, data residency, SLA).

**Trials & billing.** Every plan has a **14-day free trial**, no card required. Annual
billing saves 20%.

**Coming soon / roadmap.** Voice (business telephony), CRM and Retail (point of sale,
inventory and multi-store operations) are coming soon; Agents, Real Estate, Workshop, VPN
and Desk are on the roadmap. Custom apps are built to order on the same foundation.

**Regions.** Hosted and tuned per region, with data in your region, your language and
currency, and local payroll/compliance (e.g. WPS, GOSI, end-of-service, Nitaqat in the
Gulf): **North America** (US, Canada), **Europe** (UK, Ireland, France, Germany, Spain,
Italy, Netherlands), **GCC** (Saudi, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman), **Middle East**
(Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon, Iraq, Palestine, Syria, Yemen); other markets on request.
