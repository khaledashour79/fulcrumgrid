**Company.** FulcrumGrid is a growing family of purpose-built business apps from one
team, made by **Avenlor Consulting**. Site: **fulcrumgrid.com**. Taglines: "The
operational backbone for modern teams" and "One grid. Every operation." Contact / sales:
**contact@avenlorconsulting.com** or the demo form at **/contact/**. Pricing at **/pricing/**.
Start with one app today and add the rest as you grow — every app runs on the same
secure, auditable grid, with cross-app data when you run several.

**Live apps (3).**
- **HR Suite** — people operations from hire to retire: employee records, onboarding,
  payroll, time off, attendance, performance, and 40 modules on one grid (Core HR always
  on). Includes AI Copilot & human-approved agents. **From SAR 5 / seat / month**
  (Starter; Growth SAR 10, Enterprise SAR 18, per seat/mo).
- **Command Center** — a real-time operations dashboard / control room: live KPIs and
  custom dashboards, alerts and thresholds, automated workflows, reporting and exports,
  OKRs/projects/approvals, and ERP integrations. **From SAR 490 / month** (Pilot, flat per
  organization; Growth SAR 1,900/mo, Enterprise custom).
- **Collection** — receivables and payments, handled: invoice and ledger tracking,
  automated reminders and payment plans, reconciliation, cheque register, ERP connectors,
  aging and risk reports. **Starter is free**, then **from SAR 370 / month**
  (Professional; Business SAR 1,120/mo, Enterprise/Agency custom).

**Bundle.** Running several apps? The **whole-grid** plan covers every app at **$29 /
user / month** (unlimited users, cross-app data, priority support); **Enterprise** is
custom (single-tenant, on-premise/self-hosted option, data residency, SLA).

**Trials & billing.** Every plan has a **14-day free trial**, no card required. Annual
billing saves 20%.

**Coming soon / roadmap.** Close, TMS, Voice and Assure are coming soon; CRM, Agents,
Real Estate and Workshop are on the roadmap. Custom apps are built to order on the same
foundation.

**Regions.** Hosted and tuned per region, with data in your region, your language and
currency, and local payroll/compliance (e.g. WPS, GOSI, end-of-service, Nitaqat in the
Gulf): **North America** (US, Canada), **Europe** (UK, Ireland, France, Germany, Spain,
Italy, Netherlands), **GCC** (Saudi, UAE, Qatar, Kuwait, Bahrain, Oman), **Middle East**
(Egypt, Jordan, Lebanon, Iraq, Palestine, Syria, Yemen); other markets on request.

**Languages (7).** English, Arabic, French, German, Spanish, Italian, Dutch.
